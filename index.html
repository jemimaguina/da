01 — About

Curious by nature.

I am Jemima Guina, a director and business owner with a genuine passion for passion itself, learning and creating.


I love discovering new subjects, exploring different perspectives and challenging myself to learn things outside my comfort zone. My journey has taken me through filmmaking, entrepreneurship, fragrance, skincare, robotics and student leadership.


What connects everything I do is simple: curiosity, creativity and the desire to keep growing.

02 — Film

Four stories.

I directed my first short film at 15. Since then, filmmaking has become one of the ways I explore ideas, emotions and stories. My most recent film was created for my 18th birthday.

No More Attiéké received two awards and was selected by other festivals, marking an important step in my filmmaking journey.

03 — Robotics

My journey in robotics.

I discovered robotics in high school and became fascinated by building, testing, solving problems and working as a team.


With my school team RobotHer, I competed in a national robotics competition and we won. I later competed internationally at VEX Robotics in Texas, USA.

04 — Business

JEM'SIA.

A small business I created at 14.


JEM'SIA is my skincare and fragrance business, created from my desire to build something of my own at a young age.


Through JEM'SIA, I do my best to inspire young girls while providing quality products they can enjoy and feel confident using. I am especially interested in fragrance and often create perfumes and fragrances myself.

05 — Student Council

Learning to represent and lead.

Being part of my school's student council taught me how to communicate, listen to different perspectives, contribute ideas and take responsibility within my school community.
